# 🏴‍☠️ Grand Line Trainer

**Grand Line Trainer** is a fan-made, browser-based training simulator for the **One Piece Card Game**. Practice turns, explore card abilities, and learn the basics of playing — on desktop, phone, or tablet.

## 🎮 Play the trainer

**[▶ Open Grand Line Trainer](https://crankerr.github.io/grand-line-trainer/index.html)**

No installation is required. Open the link in your browser.

## ✨ Features

- Interactive battlefield with Character, Leader, DON!!, Life, Deck, and Trash zones
- Practice matches with card effects and in-game ability choice panels
- Long-press a card on mobile/tablet or right-click on desktop to inspect its details
- Beginner-friendly card explanations and an in-app rules quick reference
- Save/resume functionality and export/import backups
- An offline standalone HTML version for compatible browsers

## 📖 Official rules

The in-app Rules section provides a quick reference. For authoritative rules, consult the [official One Piece Card Game website](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/rules/) and its published rule documents.

## 📱 Offline version

Open [`legacy/Grand-Line-Trainer-OFFLINE.html`](legacy/Grand-Line-Trainer-OFFLINE.html) in a compatible browser to use the standalone version without hosting it.

## ⚠️ Development status

Grand Line Trainer is **a work in progress**. Some cards and effects are not fully implemented, and certain gameplay mechanics are simplified. The official rulebook takes precedence over the simulator whenever they differ.

This is an **unofficial fan project**, not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai, Toei Animation, or the owners of One Piece.
